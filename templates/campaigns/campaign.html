Wiki

Visit the wiki.

The wiki is a special notebook automatically created for the campaign. Use it to take notes, about anything. Anyone joining the campaign can make changes to it.

Link notebooks to the wiki. They will create a folder in the wiki, using the name of the notebook. If a page in the wiki was created under the same name, it will be obscured by the notebook and become inaccessible.

Private notebooks stay private. Notebooks linked to a campaign do not automatically become visible to other players, nor give them the ability to edit. You must give them explicit permission.
