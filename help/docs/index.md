# Help

Welcome to your5e, a toolkit for tabletop roleplayers and game masters. This
site provides tools for organising your campaigns, keeping notes, and sharing
resources with your group.

## Campaigns

Campaigns allow you to share content with your gaming table. Each campaign
comes with a "wiki" allowing you to group your notebooks and make them
available to your group.

Share the join URL from the campaign page with other players to invite them to
your campaign.


## Notebooks

Notebooks are folders of content, which can be shared between campaigns, and
individual players. This could be your personal character notes, the DM's
session planning, monster statblocks, and so on. See the detailed description
of [[using notebooks]].

If you are an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) user, you can sync the notebooks
available to you to your vault using the [[sync plugin]].
